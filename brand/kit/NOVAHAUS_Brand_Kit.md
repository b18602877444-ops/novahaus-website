# NOVAHAUS Brand Kit

**Brand:** NOVAHAUS  
**Positioning:** AI Brand & Digital Studio  
**Brand promise:** Clearer positioning, stronger digital experiences and practical systems for ambitious businesses.

## Brand essence

NOVAHAUS is calm, exacting and commercially minded. The identity should feel like a considered technology company with the discipline of a premium design practice: quiet confidence, useful intelligence and no unnecessary decoration.

Keywords: **Minimal · Premium · Strategic · International · Precise · Human**

## Logo usage

The primary symbol is the C2 geometric N/H monogram. It is built from a single, repeatable stroke system and should always remain crisp and unmodified.

- Use the approved C2 monogram in gold on dark fields or black on light fields.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least the width of one outer monogram stroke on every side.
- Use the horizontal lockup when the brand name is required; use the monogram alone for avatars, icons and compact applications.
- Minimum digital monogram size: 24 px. Preferred header size: 30–42 px.
- Minimum print monogram size: 8 mm.
- Never stretch, skew, outline, bevel, rotate, recolor, add glow, add a container, or place the mark over low-contrast imagery.
- Do not recreate the mark with a typeface. Use the supplied vector master.

## Color system

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Obsidian | `#0B0B0B` | Dark surfaces, primary text, premium contrast |
| Accent | Champagne Gold | `#C8A24A` | Emphasis, rules, active states, key details |
| Light | Porcelain | `#FAFAF8` | Main light backgrounds |
| White | White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed text and high-contrast surfaces |
| Neutral | Stone | `#77736A` | Supporting copy and metadata |

Gold is an accent, not a fill for large areas. Keep the palette mostly obsidian and porcelain; use gold sparingly so it retains authority.

## Typography

- **Display / headlines:** Space Grotesk, 500–600. Use compact line-height and generous surrounding space.
- **Body / UI:** Inter, 400–600. Use 150–165% line-height for longer copy.
- **Labels:** Inter, 600, uppercase, 0.12–0.18em tracking.
- **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

Use sentence case for communication. Reserve all caps for short navigational labels, eyebrow text and metadata.

## Icon style

Use small, geometric, monoline icons with square or subtly rounded terminals. Icons should explain an action or capability, never act as decoration. Use black, white or Champagne Gold only. Avoid robots, brains, circuit boards and generic sparkle symbols.

## Button style

- Height: 48–52 px for primary actions; 40–44 px for compact actions.
- Radius: 999 px for pill CTAs or 12 px for utility controls, used consistently within a product.
- Primary: Obsidian fill with porcelain text on light surfaces; porcelain fill with obsidian text on dark surfaces.
- Accent: Gold is reserved for focus rings, small rules and selected states.
- Hover: translateY(-1 px), slightly deeper shadow, no dramatic scale.
- Focus: 2 px Champagne Gold outline with 3 px offset.

## Card style

Cards use a quiet surface, a 1 px neutral border and a restrained shadow. Standard radius is 20–24 px. Keep internal padding generous and align every card to the same baseline grid. The card should clarify a decision, not imitate a dashboard.

## Image style

Prefer architectural compositions, material details, editorial portraits, abstract systems and quiet moments of work. Use directional light, controlled contrast and generous negative space. Avoid stock-photo smiles, glossy 3D device mockups, neon gradients and generic “AI” imagery.

## Illustration style

Illustrations should be sparse and diagrammatic: grids, measured lines, modular shapes and carefully placed points. Use the brand palette, one visual idea at a time. A useful illustration should make a system easier to understand.

## Social and presentation usage

Keep the monogram optically centered, leave safe space for platform cropping and never place critical copy at the extreme edges. Use one message per asset. The primary message should be legible without zooming on a phone.

## Quality checklist

- Is the C2 monogram unmodified and high contrast?
- Is gold being used as emphasis rather than decoration?
- Is there enough negative space around the message?
- Does the layout read clearly in a one-second scan?
- Does the asset feel calm, exact and commercially useful?
