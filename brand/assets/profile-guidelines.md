# NOVAHAUS Social Profile Guidelines

## Profile image

Use the approved C2 gold monogram on an Obsidian background for LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and Xiaohongshu profile images. The mark should remain legible at small sizes and should not be combined with a long wordmark inside a circular crop.

## Naming

Use **NOVAHAUS** consistently across platforms. Do not add “Studio”, “AI Lab” or other suffixes unless the channel strategy changes and the naming is updated across every profile.

## Voice

NOVAHAUS sounds:

- strategic, not abstract;
- confident, not exaggerated;
- useful, not mechanical;
- international, not overly corporate;
- commercially aware, not sales-heavy.

Prefer:

> Make the important path easier.

Avoid:

> Unlock the future with revolutionary AI innovation.

## Content principles

1. Start with a real business problem.
2. Explain the decision or system clearly.
3. Show the practical next step.
4. Avoid invented client results, certifications and partnerships.
5. Do not promise guaranteed leads, revenue, growth or investment returns.

## Visual system

- Use Obsidian, Porcelain, White, Stone and Champagne Gold.
- Prefer a quiet editorial layout with one strong idea.
- Use architectural grids, measured lines and modular shapes for diagrams.
- Avoid generic robots, circuit-board graphics, neon, esports styling and excessive glass effects.

## Accessibility and quality

- Check text contrast before publishing.
- Add meaningful alt text to every image.
- Keep important information in text, not only inside an image.
- Test crops on both desktop and mobile.
- Use captions on video and keep the first frame understandable without sound.
