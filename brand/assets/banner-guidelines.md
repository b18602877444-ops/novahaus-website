## Composition

- Keep critical copy inside the central safe area; platforms crop banners differently across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Use one primary message per asset.
- Preserve generous negative space around the C2 monogram and NOVAHAUS wordmark.
- Do not place contact details, legal copy or small text at the extreme edges.
- Check every export on a phone before publishing.

## Logo

- Use the approved C2 geometric N/H monogram.
- Use the horizontal logo only when the full wordmark is required.
- Do not redraw, stretch, rotate, glow, bevel or add a container to the logo.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least one outer monogram stroke.

## Color

- Obsidian: `#0B0B0B`
- Champagne Gold: `#C8A24A`
- Porcelain: `#FAFAF8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Stone: `#77736A`

Use gold as a restrained accent. Avoid large gold fills, neon effects, cheap gradients and cyberpunk styling.

## Export

- Prefer SVG for future editing and layout adaptation.
- Use PNG for direct platform upload.
- Export in sRGB.
- Keep text as vector or high-resolution raster at the final platform size.
- Review contrast and legibility at 25% scale.
