# NOVAHAUS Product Catalog

NOVAHAUS products are structured starting points for businesses that need clearer positioning, stronger digital experiences and practical AI systems. Scope is confirmed around the client’s context, priorities and operating environment. No product includes guaranteed leads, revenue, growth or investment returns.

## Product 01 — Launch

### Target Customer

New businesses, founders, professional practices and established teams preparing to launch, reposition or enter a new market.

### Business Problems Solved

- The offer is difficult to explain quickly.
- The brand and website do not create enough confidence.
- Key audiences are unclear or poorly prioritised.
- The business lacks a focused route from attention to enquiry.
- Existing content and digital assets feel disconnected.

### Deliverables

- Positioning and message direction.
- Core brand and visual direction.
- Conversion-focused website structure.
- Responsive website or landing page system.
- Key page and CTA copy direction.
- Lead capture and enquiry pathway.
- Foundational SEO and analytics planning.
- Handover guidance for the next stage.

### Included AI Features

- AI-assisted content and messaging exploration.
- Guided AI Sales Agent entry point where appropriate.
- Growth Assessment pathway.
- AI opportunity mapping during strategy.
- Proposal preparation from approved business context.

AI outputs are reviewed before they are used in important business, legal, financial or operational decisions.

### Timeline

Usually delivered as a focused launch engagement over several weeks. Final timing depends on decision speed, content readiness, feedback cycles and technical scope.

### Onboarding Process

1. Complete a Strategy Call request.
2. Share the current business context, audience and primary goal.
3. Confirm scope, responsibilities, timing and working process.
4. Complete the discovery and positioning phase.
5. Review the proposed direction before production begins.

### Upgrade Path

Move to **Growth** when the business needs stronger automation, lead management, analytics or ongoing optimisation after the foundation is live.

### FAQ

#### Is Launch only for new businesses?

No. It also suits existing businesses that need to reposition, simplify their offer or replace a digital presence that no longer reflects the business.

#### Does Launch include a full brand identity?

The exact identity scope is confirmed during discovery. The product includes the brand direction needed to create a coherent digital foundation.

#### Can AI features be added later?

Yes. Launch is structured so automation, AI Sales Agent and other growth systems can be added without rebuilding the foundation.

#### What happens after launch?

The team can continue independently, move into Growth or discuss a longer-term partnership based on the next business priority.

## Product 02 — Growth

### Target Customer

Businesses with an active offer, existing demand or a live digital presence that need better lead quality, clearer customer journeys and less manual operational work.

### Business Problems Solved

- Enquiries arrive without enough context.
- Follow-up is inconsistent across channels.
- CRM, forms and internal workflows are disconnected.
- The website attracts attention but does not guide the next step.
- Teams cannot easily see where prospects are getting stuck.
- Content, analytics and optimisation happen without a consistent rhythm.

### Deliverables

- Growth diagnosis and prioritised roadmap.
- Conversion-focused website or journey improvements.
- AI Sales Agent and structured lead capture.
- Lead qualification and service recommendation logic.
- CRM-ready data structure and workflow planning.
- Proposal and strategy-call pathways.
- Content and knowledge-system direction.
- Analytics and conversion review framework.
- Post-launch optimisation recommendations.

### Included AI Features

- AI Sales Agent for guided discovery.
- Lead qualification and structured summaries.
- Service and package recommendations.
- AI opportunity mapping across the customer journey.
- AI-assisted content system planning.
- Proposal preparation from conversation and assessment context.
- Workflow opportunities for follow-up and internal operations.

AI features support human decision-making and require appropriate review, governance and approval.

### Timeline

Delivered in staged phases over several weeks or months, depending on the number of journeys, integrations, content requirements and review cycles.

### Onboarding Process

1. Begin with a Strategy Call or Growth Assessment.
2. Review existing website, lead flow, tools and operating constraints.
3. Prioritise the highest-value journey or workflow.
4. Confirm the delivery roadmap and information required from the team.
5. Build, test and review each system layer before launch.
6. Establish a measurement and optimisation rhythm.

### Upgrade Path

Move to **Enterprise** when the business needs multiple markets, complex integrations, custom workflows, deeper governance or ongoing strategic support across teams.

### FAQ

#### Do we need an existing CRM?

No. Growth can begin with a CRM-ready architecture and connect to an approved CRM when the business is ready.

#### Can Growth include website improvements rather than a new website?

Yes. The scope can focus on the existing journey where that is the most practical route.

#### Does Growth guarantee more leads or revenue?

No. NOVAHAUS improves clarity, experience and operating systems. Commercial outcomes depend on the business, market, offer and implementation context.

#### Can the work be delivered in stages?

Yes. The roadmap is prioritised so the business can launch the most useful system layer first and expand deliberately.

## Product 03 — Enterprise

### Target Customer

Complex organisations, multi-market businesses, leadership teams and established companies planning significant digital transformation, automation or international expansion.

### Business Problems Solved

- Multiple teams communicate the offer differently.
- Customer journeys vary across markets and channels.
- Existing systems do not share reliable context.
- Manual workflows create operational drag at scale.
- Leadership lacks a shared roadmap for AI adoption.
- Governance, access and approval requirements are increasing.
- Growth initiatives compete without a clear sequence.

### Deliverables

- Executive growth and digital systems diagnosis.
- Enterprise positioning and message architecture.
- Multi-market website and experience system.
- AI Sales Agent and tailored lead qualification.
- CRM, API and integration architecture planning.
- Knowledge Hub and approved content structure.
- Custom workflow and automation design.
- Analytics, governance and optimisation framework.
- Phased transformation roadmap.
- Ongoing strategic partnership option.

### Included AI Features

- Configurable AI Sales Agent journeys.
- Role- and stage-aware lead qualification.
- Service, market and next-step recommendations.
- Approved Knowledge Hub experience.
- AI-assisted workflow and operations mapping.
- Proposal and briefing preparation from structured context.
- AI governance, review and escalation planning.

AI capabilities are designed around approved data, defined permissions and human oversight. External AI, CRM, API, calendar, email or messaging integrations are confirmed separately.

### Timeline

Planned as a phased transformation. Timing depends on organisational complexity, markets, integrations, content governance, stakeholder availability and implementation priorities.

### Onboarding Process

1. Begin with an executive Strategy Call.
2. Map business goals, markets, teams, systems and constraints.
3. Identify priority journeys and operational opportunities.
4. Define governance, ownership, access and approval requirements.
5. Agree the transformation roadmap and delivery phases.
6. Build, validate and roll out each system with the relevant teams.
7. Review performance and refine the operating model.

### Upgrade Path

Enterprise is the highest NOVAHAUS product tier. Ongoing work can expand through additional markets, journeys, integrations, workflow layers and strategic support, subject to a new scope review.

### FAQ

#### Is Enterprise only for large companies?

No. It is for organisations with complex journeys, multiple stakeholders or transformation requirements that need more than a focused launch or growth engagement.

#### Can Enterprise work with our existing tools?

The architecture can be designed around existing tools and future integrations. Access, security, ownership and technical feasibility are reviewed before implementation.

#### Does Enterprise include custom development?

Custom development may be included when it supports the agreed system architecture. It is scoped separately from the strategic direction.

#### Can we start with one department or market?

Yes. A controlled starting point can reduce risk and provide a practical foundation for later expansion.

#### How are AI outputs governed?

Each use case should define approved sources, human review, escalation rules, access boundaries and the decisions AI is not permitted to make.

## Choosing a Product

- Choose **Launch** when the business needs a clear, credible foundation.
- Choose **Growth** when the business needs better conversion, qualification and operational leverage.
- Choose **Enterprise** when the business needs a coordinated, scalable transformation system.

If the right starting point is unclear, book a NOVAHAUS Strategy Call. The recommendation will be based on the business context rather than a fixed sales script.
